🏋 Your 3-day gym programme

Designed for body recomposition · weights first · around 45 minutes per session. Rotate A, B and C with at least one recovery day between sessions where possible.

WORKOUT A · UPPER BODY
Warm-up: 5 min easy treadmill or bike, then 1 light warm-up set on your first two machines.
1. Seated chest press
3 sets × 8–12 reps · Rest 90 sec
Alternative: dumbbell bench press.
2. Seated cable row
3 × 8–12 · Rest 75–90 sec
Alternative: chest-supported row machine.
3. Lat pulldown
3 × 10–12 · Rest 75 sec
Alternative: assisted pull-up machine.
4. Machine shoulder press
2 × 10–12 · Rest 75 sec
Alternative: seated dumbbell shoulder press.
5. Cable triceps pushdown + dumbbell biceps curl
2 × 10–12 each · Rest 45–60 sec
Alternate the two exercises to save time.
WORKOUT B · LOWER BODY & CORE
Warm-up: 5 min bike, then 1 light warm-up set on leg press.
1. Leg press
3 × 10–12 · Rest 90 sec
Alternative: goblet squat to a comfortable depth.
2. Seated hamstring curl
3 × 10–12 · Rest 75 sec
Alternative: lying hamstring curl.
3. Leg extension
2 × 10–12 · Rest 60 sec
Alternative: supported split squat, bodyweight to start.
4. Glute drive / hip thrust machine
2 × 10–12 · Rest 75 sec
Alternative: glute bridge.
5. Plank
3 × 20–40 sec · Rest 45 sec
Alternative: dead bug, 3 × 8 each side.
WORKOUT C · FULL BODY
Warm-up: 5 min cross-trainer or treadmill, then 1 light warm-up set.
1. Goblet squat
3 × 10 · Rest 90 sec
Alternative: leg press.
2. Incline chest press machine
3 × 8–12 · Rest 90 sec
Alternative: incline dumbbell press.
3. Chest-supported row
3 × 10–12 · Rest 75 sec
Alternative: seated cable row.
4. Dumbbell Romanian deadlift
2 × 10 · Rest 90 sec
Alternative: cable pull-through. Keep the back neutral.
5. Cable woodchop
2 × 10 each side · Rest 45 sec
Alternative: dead bug, 2 × 10 each side.

How to choose your weights: no safe starting weight can be guessed from body weight. For each exercise, start very light and increase gradually until you can complete the target reps with good form while feeling you could still do 2–3 more reps. Record that weight. If you cannot reach the minimum reps cleanly, reduce it. When you reach the top of the rep range on every set with good form, increase by the smallest available increment next time.

Cardio finisher: optional 5–8 minutes brisk walking, bike or cross-trainer at a pace where you can still speak. Skip it if time or recovery is poor. If you're unusually exhausted, unwell or severely sleep-deprived, choose a lighter session or rest.

The plan is a starting programme, not a substitute for coaching on technique. Stop if an exercise causes sharp pain.
